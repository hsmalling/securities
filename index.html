📡 How It Works
Each symbol is scored across 13 timeframes: Today, Overnight (OC), Overnight C↔C, 5D, Week, Past Wk, Pw C↔C, Month, Past Mo, Pm C↔C, 6Mo, YTD, 1Yr.
Each timeframe returns BUY, SELL, or AVOID.
Confidence = how many technical factors align (RSI, MACD, MA stack, ADX, structure, volume).
All filters combine with AND — every active filter must match.
